About

Kuro Library is a personal Android application designed to help you organize your digital manga and reading collection.

Keep your files organized, manage your library, and enjoy having your collection in one place.

Features

- 📚 Organize your digital reading collection.
- 📁 Keep your files arranged in folders.
- 📱 Access your library on Android.
- 🗂️ Manage your personal collection of reading files.

Features and supported file formats may vary by version.
